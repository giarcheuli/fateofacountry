# Country Monitoring System: Source Strategy

**Status:** Selection criteria approved; source roster research incomplete.  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-26

## Direction

The initial source base should be small (about 3–5 sources) and credible, with broad coverage over time. The project owner asked to identify a larger candidate pool of about 20, weighted toward:

- Government statistical publications and official datasets.
- Ministries, parliamentary and statutory agency publications, and public records.
- Independent research centers and civil-society organizations publishing transparent research.

Source-type priority does not imply that every institution or publication is neutral. The product must attribute claims to their sources, preserve conflicting evidence, and avoid treating institutional status or an AI extraction as proof of truth.

## Evaluation criteria for each candidate

Record the institution and exact publication/feed URL; topic coverage; primary/secondary source type; update frequency; Georgian/English availability; machine-readable access (RSS/API/HTML/PDF); archive stability; citation and reuse/licensing terms; known access or extraction limitations; and rationale for inclusion. Prefer original data and reports. Keep this source evaluation separate from the assessment of individual claims.
