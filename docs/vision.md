# Country Monitoring System: Vision

**Status:** Approved project direction  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-26

## Purpose

Build an accessible, evidence-linked platform that helps people understand important developments in Georgia by organizing claims from journalism, public records, research, and other documented sources. The system should preserve where each claim came from, show supporting and contradicting evidence, communicate uncertainty, and keep a visible history when a claim changes or is retracted.

## Product principles

- Start with Georgia and a small, maintainable source set.
- Treat a claim with its evidence and version history as the basic reviewable unit.
- AI assists with extraction and organization; it does not independently verify a claim or replace human review.
- Require human approval before information is published.
- Keep evidence traceable and uncertainty visible. Never silently overwrite published history.
- Use open-source-compatible tools and keep the project noncommercial and community-maintained.
- Any public prototype must be clearly labeled experimental and explain its limitations.

## Intended outcome

A working end-to-end prototype demonstrates collecting a real Georgian source, extracting a candidate claim, reviewing it, publishing it with evidence, then updating and retracting it while preserving the history.

## Scope boundary

The initial product is a Georgia-focused public information analysis tool, not an automated truth adjudicator, a general-purpose news feed, or a system that publishes model output without review. Additional countries, autonomous topic discovery, advanced credibility scoring, and large-scale analytics are post-MVP ideas.
